## Welcome!

I am PhD student in Aerospace Engineering at the Institute for Systems and Robotics, Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa.

I am interested in autonomous spacecraft navigation and control in systems of multiple celestial bodies, with current emphasis on applications for continuous, low-magnitude thrust via electric propulsion.

Here, I do my best to open-source my research and provide free, reproducible implementations of the solutions proposed in the work I publish. In my [webpage](https://antoniownunes.github.io), you can find some additional information about me, contacts, past and ongoing projects, and my scientific output.

Feel free to contact me if you have any questions regarding my projects, publications, or any other topic.
